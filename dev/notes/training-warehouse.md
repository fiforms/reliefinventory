# Training warehouse

For a hands-on class: 6 stations (1 Receiving, 2 Sorting, 3 Inventory, 4 Picking, 5 Shipping, 6 Delivery/Ordering), 5 teams, two 50-minute segments; team N starts at station N and every team moves on one station each slot (6 → 1). `App\Support\TrainingGuide` is the single source for the printed team sheets.

## Training is a warehouse, not a mode (MULTI_WAREHOUSE.md §12)
- An ordinary warehouse with `is_training` (`warehouse:create "<name>" --training`); the only place class features exist. `TrainingMode::active()` (current warehouse is training) is the one test.
- Off = the warehouse's `suspended` status (`warehouse:training disable|enable <id>`): it refuses everything and leaves the chooser; data kept.
- In a real warehouse the class tools are inert: `HasPermissions` drops `manage-training`, the activity log records nothing, `/join` and `/training-login` don't open, Prepare Training refuses, the Trainee role is hidden. The schema is the same everywhere (same migrations), just empty outside a training warehouse.

## Pieces
- **Tap-login** (`TrainingLoginController`, `Auth/TrainingLogin.vue`): an approved `TrustedDevice` sends `/login` to `/training-login`; trainee name tiles by team, tap → confirm team → in, no password; **Add New** creates one. A trainee has what the Trainee role grants (`TrainingMode::TRAINING_KEYS`). Trainees are `people.is_trainee` + `training_team`, placeholder `trainee-<id>@training.invalid`, no account.
- **Reset** (`TrainingReset`, `training:reset`, `warehouse:training reset <id>`, Setup > Training Warehouse, typed `RESET`): drops and rebuilds that one warehouse with its own DB user (status `restoring`). First a class archive (sign-ups CSV, report cards PDF+CSV, certificates) is saved to the warehouse's files, which a reset keeps along with `training-signatures/`. A snapshot file in `<backup dir>/warehouses/<id>/training/` (outside the database, so a crash is finished by re-running) keeps who logs in (name, account, roles) and certificate details. Everything else goes. On failure the warehouse stays `restoring`, reason in `reset-status.json`.
- **Activity log** (`activity_log`, `ActivityRecorder`): training warehouses only, from model events (Transaction created/status, ItemLedger fills/sorted rows, PalletStockAssignment, W-pallet PalletStatus, donation offer log), not controller edits. Actor = `Auth::id()`; nothing logged with nobody signed in except a Driver Portal BOL upload (session `driver_id`). Team is snapshotted from the trainee's (or team driver's `drivers.training_team`). Never throws.
- **Report cards** (`TrainingReportController`, `/reports/training`): per trainee per station in a date range; a station is done with ≥1 action; a team driver's delivery counts for the whole team. Certificates (landscape PDF) for trainees who did all six, with the event title/location from `training_settings` (`PUT /json/training/event`).
- **Training products** (`/setup/training/products`, `TrainingProductController`, `TrainingProducts.vue`): the trainer scans the real goods that start "already in the warehouse" (stations 3–5), one unit per scan (`UpcResolver`, catalog only; unknown barcode opens `Components/NewItemModal.vue`). `training_products` is a list of props, **not stock**.
- **Prepare Training** (`TrainingPreparer`; refused outside training and with fewer scanned units than `plan()['target']`): stages only the first team per station (`config/training.php` `stage`). Station 1: pending offers only. 2: nothing. 3: scanned goods sorted into the sorting area. 4: sealed W pallets at table spots `A-1`…`C-4` (`spotCodes()`), plus every unit beyond the target, and Ready to Fill orders they cover. 5: Filled orders. 6: nothing. Every scanned unit appears in the summary exactly once (tested). One transaction, ActivityRecorder paused; retires old `TR-*` locations. Output (`training_settings.prepared_summary`): the setup packet, training pallet labels (`PalletReportController::trainingLabelsBuilder`), table spot labels (`training-spot-labels`). Running again adds another set.
- **Self sign-up** (`/join`, `TraineeSignupController`, `Join.vue`, public; QR + poster + follow-up CSV on the Training Warehouse page): training warehouses only; first + last name required. Makes a trainee via `createTrainee` (same name updates that person); answers live in `training_registrations`. Never logs anyone in. POST limit is 80/min per IP because a whole room shares one Wi-Fi IP; Turnstile is the bot gate — don't tighten it.
- **Print documents**: `training-report-cards`, `training-certificates`, `training-setup-packet`, `training-pallet-labels` (manage-training); `training-team-{1..5}` / `training-all-teams` (anyone signed in, listed on `/help`).
- The Driver Portal shows staff (manage-orders) the staff view, so Round 2 delivery is done on a device not logged in.
